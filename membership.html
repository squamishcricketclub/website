Membership & Registration

Choose the participation option that fits you, then complete the club’s secure external registration form.

Membership benefits

Members can access organized sessions, club communications, shared equipment where available, volunteer opportunities and a welcoming cricket community.

How to register

  1. Review the program information and current schedule.
  2. Open the external Jotform registration page.
  3. Choose the appropriate category and complete required consent or waiver information.
  4. Submit payment if applicable.
  5. Watch for a confirmation message from the club.

Jotform is a third-party service with its own privacy practices. Review the form and Jotform notices before submitting personal information.
