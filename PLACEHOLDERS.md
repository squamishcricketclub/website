# Launch content checklist

Replace and verify every bracketed item and every `REPLACE` string.

- Registered and public physical or mailing address, postal code, and map location
- BC society number and exact legal classification
- Charitable registration number, or an accurate statement that the club is not a registered charity
- Current Board names, offices, biographies, and terms
- Current membership fees, categories, eligibility, refund terms, and KidSport details
- Production Jotform URL(s), form ownership, privacy settings, retention, and confirmation workflow
- Privacy Officer name, role, email, address, retention schedule, and policy approval date
- Terms approval date
- Approved constitution, bylaws, annual reports, financial summaries, and governance policies in `/documents/`
- Verified 2026–2027 fixtures, training times, venues, opponents, and weather cancellation process
- Approved sponsor names, logos, links, recognition commitments, and image alt text
- Official Facebook and Instagram URLs
- Approved photography with releases and descriptive alt text
- Confirm founder history and Cricket BC membership wording
- Domain canonical URLs if not deploying at `https://squamishcricket.ca/`
- Contact form handling: current implementation opens the visitor’s email app; connect an approved privacy-reviewed static form service only if needed
