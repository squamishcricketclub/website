Mission & Vision

Our purpose and long-term commitments guide every program, partnership and decision.

Mission statement

Squamish Cricket Club advances community well-being by providing safe, inclusive and affordable opportunities for people of all ages and abilities to learn, play and enjoy cricket in Squamish and the Sea to Sky.

Vision

A connected community where cricket is accessible, valued and sustained by strong volunteer leadership, quality programs and appropriate local facilities.

Values

Inclusion

We welcome people across cultures, backgrounds, ages and playing experience.

Respect

We act with sportsmanship, fairness and care for participants, officials and neighbours.

Safety

We prioritize appropriate supervision, equipment, facilities and conduct.

Community

We build belonging through service, partnership and shared participation.

Goals and community development objectives

  • Offer regular adult, youth and introductory cricket programs.
  • Reduce financial and equipment barriers to participation.
  • Develop volunteer coaches, officials and club leaders.
  • Partner with schools and community organizations on outreach.
  • Support safe, sustainable cricket facilities in Squamish.
  • Maintain transparent governance and responsible financial stewardship.
