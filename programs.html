Programs & Cricket Activities

From first-time players to experienced cricketers, our programs build skills, confidence and connection.

Adult cricket

Outdoor practices, friendly matches and competitive development for adults of varied ability. Shared equipment is available for new participants.

Typical season: April to September

Youth cricket

Fun, age-appropriate sessions for ages 6–15, emphasizing movement, basic technique, teamwork and confidence. Parents or guardians complete registration and consent.

Typical season: Spring and summer

Skills training

Structured coaching in batting, bowling, fielding, game awareness and safe play. Sessions may be adapted by experience level.

Community events

Free try-cricket days, demonstrations, school outreach and cultural community events that introduce the sport.

Recreational opportunities

Low-pressure social play for people who want exercise, connection and enjoyment without a competitive commitment.

Participate safely

Schedules depend on field availability, weather and volunteer capacity. Participants must follow club rules, waivers and applicable safety requirements.
