About Us

A volunteer-led club growing inclusive cricket opportunities in Squamish and the Sea to Sky.

Our history

Squamish Cricket Club was founded in 2021 by local residents Rudi Botha, Taurean McCarthy and Ben Cole after they identified a need for organized cricket in Squamish. The club established training, attracted players and played its first fixtures in 2022. It became a sustaining member of Cricket BC in May 2022.

Community impact

We create welcoming opportunities for adults and young people to be active, learn cricket and form friendships. Our volunteer coaches and organizers support beginner-friendly sessions, school outreach, friendly competition and community events.

Our vision

A thriving, inclusive cricket community with safe, sustainable facilities and opportunities for every player to participate, develop and belong.
