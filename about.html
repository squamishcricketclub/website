About & Governance

Our history, community purpose and public society information.

About the club

Squamish Cricket Club was founded in 2021 to create organized cricket opportunities in Squamish. Volunteers operate programs for adults, youth and community members.

Community benefit

The club supports active living, skill development, teamwork and social connection. Beginners are welcome, and shared equipment may be provided where available.

Vision and values

Our vision is a sustainable and inclusive cricket community in the Sea to Sky. Our values are safety, respect, sportsmanship, inclusion, service and responsible stewardship.
