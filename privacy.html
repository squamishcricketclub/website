Privacy Policy

How personal information is collected, used and protected.

Effective: [APPROVAL DATE]

Accountability

The Board designates a Privacy Officer. Contact: [INSERT].

Collection and purposes

The club may collect contact, registration, consent, emergency, attendance, payment-status and volunteer information reasonably needed to deliver programs and meet legal, safety or insurance obligations.

Consent and youth

Meaningful consent is sought where required. A parent or guardian provides required consent for youth participants.

Service providers

Jotform, email, payment, hosting or storage providers may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Safeguards and retention

Access is limited according to role, records are protected appropriately, and information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary.

Access and complaints

Contact the Privacy Officer to request access, correction or review of a privacy concern, subject to lawful exceptions.
