Privacy

How the club handles personal information.

Effective date: TO BE CONFIRMED

Accountability

The Board designates a Privacy Officer: TO BE CONFIRMED.

Collection and purposes

The club may collect contact, registration, consent, emergency-contact, attendance, payment-status and volunteer information reasonably needed to operate programs, communicate, support safety and meet legal or insurance obligations.

Consent and youth information

The club seeks consent where required. A parent or legal guardian provides required consent for youth participants.

Service providers

Jotform and approved email, payment, hosting or storage providers may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Use, retention and security

The club does not sell personal information. Access is limited, safeguards are used according to sensitivity, and information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary.

Access and questions

Contact squamishcricketclub@gmail.com to request access or correction or to raise a privacy concern, subject to lawful exceptions.
