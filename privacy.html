Privacy Policy

How Squamish Cricket Club handles personal information.

Effective date: [INSERT APPROVAL DATE]

This policy is a practical website draft for review by the Board and is not legal advice.

1. Accountability

Squamish Cricket Club is responsible for personal information under its control. The Board will designate a Privacy Officer. Contact: [PRIVACY OFFICER EMAIL AND MAILING ADDRESS].

2. Information we collect

Depending on your relationship with the club, we may collect contact details, age or date of birth, emergency contacts, registration and payment status, consent and waiver records, attendance, volunteer screening status, photographs with permission, and communications.

3. Purposes

We collect information reasonably needed to administer membership and programs, communicate schedules and safety notices, respond to enquiries, manage volunteers, meet legal or insurance obligations, process authorized payments, and report aggregate program outcomes.

4. Consent and youth information

We seek meaningful consent where required. A parent or legal guardian must provide required consent for youth participants. Consent may be withdrawn subject to legal, safety and operational limits.

5. Service providers and disclosure

We may use service providers such as Jotform, email, payment, hosting and document-storage providers. They may process information outside British Columbia or Canada. We do not sell personal information. We disclose it only with consent or as permitted or required by law.

6. Limiting collection, use and retention

We limit collection to reasonable purposes, restrict use to identified or compatible purposes, and retain records only as long as needed for legal, insurance, safety and operational requirements. The Board will approve a retention schedule.

7. Safeguards

We use measures appropriate to sensitivity, including access controls, account security, limited volunteer access, secure disposal and reasonable vendor review. No online system is completely secure.

8. Accuracy, access and correction

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, subject to lawful exceptions. Contact the Privacy Officer in writing. We may verify identity before responding.

9. Website data

This static website does not intentionally set advertising cookies. Hosting providers may process technical logs such as IP address, browser and request time for security and delivery. If analytics are added, this policy and consent approach must be reviewed first.

10. Questions and complaints

Contact the Privacy Officer first. You may also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date will show the latest approved version.
